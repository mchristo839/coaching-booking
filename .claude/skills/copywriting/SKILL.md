# Copywriting

Turn flat or generic web copy into specific, confident, conversion-focused text.

## Process
1. Read the existing copy and the product context (what it does, who it is for, the one thing it does better than alternatives).
2. Rewrite each piece against the rules below.
3. Show the before and after side by side so the user can pick.

## Rules
- **Specific beats clever.** "Cut invoice chasing from 6 hours to 20 minutes" beats "Streamline your workflow."
- **Lead with the benefit,** then the feature. The reader cares what changes for them.
- **Kill filler:** "seamlessly", "revolutionary", "next-generation", "empower", "leverage", "solutions". Replace with concrete words.
- **Headlines** state the outcome, not the category. Avoid "The future of X" and "X, reimagined."
- **CTAs** describe the action and value ("Get my free audit"), not "Submit" or "Learn more."
- **One idea per section.** Short sentences. Active voice.
- Match the brand voice in DESIGN.md. Confident, not hype.

## Output
For each block: the original, the rewrite, and a one-line note on why it is stronger.
