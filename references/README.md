# references/

Drop finished example pages here. These are your "what good looks like" examples.

## Why this folder exists
The design system (`DESIGN.md`) is the recipe. A reference page is the finished dish.
The recipe alone often does not carry the harder parts (animation, layout polish, the feel),
so giving Claude a real example to match raises the quality fast.

## What to put here
- A saved `.html` page you admire (View Source, or save the page) renamed clearly, e.g. `hero-reference.html`.
- A screenshot (`.png`) of a design you want to match.
- A short note (`.md`) describing what you like about each one, e.g. "love the tight headline tracking and the dark premium feel."

## How to use it
When you ask Claude Code to build, point it here:
"Build the landing page. Match the quality and structure of references/hero-reference.html, but use our DESIGN.md colours and type."

## Building your taste library over time
Whenever you see a site, app, or section you love, save it here.
This is your design "second brain." The more good references you collect,
the better and less generic your output gets.
